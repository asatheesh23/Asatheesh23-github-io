About

Biography

I am a Computer Science academic with 24+ years of experience spanning higher education and the software industry. I currently serve as Dean of the College of Computing at Euro University of Bahrain, where I also teach on the Goldsmiths, University of London BSc Computer Science programme.

My academic career has taken me across Bahrain, India and Taiwan, combining senior academic leadership with collaborative research involving institutions in the UK, Taiwan, Japan and India. I completed postdoctoral research at National Taipei University's Research Center on Big Data and Smart City, working on externally funded machine-learning research in environmental forecasting.

I contributed as the India partner to the EPSRC UK–India Future Networks Initiative pilot project on APT Threat Detection in 5G Networks, in collaboration with the University of Surrey. My established research profile spans Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, Edge Intelligence, IoT and Cybersecurity, with more than 37 peer-reviewed publications including recent Q1/SCI-indexed outputs.

I am a Fellow of Advance HE (FHEA) and received the ISTE National Award for Best Research Work by Young Teachers in 2010. I am currently targeting Dean, Head of School or Professorial appointments where academic governance, programme accreditation and applied AI research leadership come together in one mandate.
